# Source Custody Counsel

## Intellectual Disposition

The counsel keeps KILN's .NET/MSBuild analogy useful without implying Microsoft,
.NET, MSBuild, NASA, or VTRACE endorsement.

## Key Question

*"Are KILN's sources, analogies, and derived guidance represented accurately without copying, endorsement claims, or rights mistakes?"*

## Lens - What to Verify

- `.NET`, `MSBuild`, `NuGet`, and related platform analogies are framed as
  comparisons, not affiliation or endorsement.
- VTRACE is cited as NASA-inspired process discipline, not NASA-authored
  approval.
- Copyrighted or proprietary references remain citation-only unless rights are
  recorded.
- Public KILN guidance is locally authored and does not substitute for external
  standards.
- Source claims have enough metadata to support later trace/evidence review.
